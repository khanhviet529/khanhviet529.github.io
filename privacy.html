Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

What we access

When the owner connects TikTok, the app receives an access token and the basic profile information allowed by the granted scopes (open ID, display name, avatar) so that it can upload videos on the owner's behalf.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by us and are not shared with third parties. The tool has no database, no analytics and no advertising.

Your control

The owner can disconnect at any time from the app, delete the local token file, or revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.

Children

The tool is not directed to children under 13.
